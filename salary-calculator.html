How Salary Is Calculated

Your salary is converted between pay periods by working out how many hours or periods fit into a year based on your schedule. This calculator uses your weekly hours and weeks worked per year to determine your true hourly rate, then scales that rate up or down to show every common pay period.

Annual, Monthly, Weekly & Hourly Pay Explained

  • Annual Income: Your base salary plus any overtime and bonus income for the full year.
  • Monthly Income: Annual income divided by 12 calendar months.
  • Biweekly Income: Annual income divided by 26 pay periods (every two weeks).
  • Weekly Income: Annual income divided by the number of weeks you work per year.
  • Daily Income: Weekly income divided by 5 working days.
  • Hourly Wage: Your base annual salary divided by total hours worked per year (weekly hours × weeks per year).

How Overtime Pay Is Calculated

Overtime pay is calculated by multiplying your hourly wage by your chosen overtime multiplier — typically 1.5× for time-and-a-half — and then by your weekly overtime hours and the number of weeks you work per year. This amount is added on top of your base salary in the total estimated annual income.

Why Weeks Per Year Matters

Not everyone works a full 52-week year. Unpaid leave, seasonal work, or unpaid holidays can reduce the number of weeks actually worked, which changes your effective hourly and weekly pay even if your annual salary stays the same. Adjusting this field gives a more accurate picture of your real pay rate.
